# Campaign Slate — CONCEPTS (FROZEN)

**Status: FROZEN — 2026-08-06.** Ten part-design campaigns, one per domain, for the LMCAD engine. Both adversarial reviews (the diversity-complexity judge and the engine-fit skeptic) have been applied: three re-steers executed (electronics DIN foot, optics architecture, agriculture indexing), one disputed pick adjudicated and kept with hardening (acoustics), and every skeptic repair folded into the card it names — including one blocking repair (automotive clamp). No runner-up promotions were made: the skeptic verified every runner-up rejection as sound, and no killed element had a live runner-up. Any change after this point requires an explicit unfreeze note.

## The bar

Every card below clears the complicated-part bar:

- **(a)** ≥3 mating interfaces against real published dimensioned standards, each with a `tolerance_stack.py` proof at ±0.15 mm FDM extremes;
- **(b)** a genuine numeric load path honestly covered by analyses inside their documented validity — Validated tier where claimable; in-house-gated and cataloged tiers labeled every time they are quoted; fillet/notch peaks as closed-form Kt × FEA nominal (never the voxel peak); PLA honesty throughout (creep table governs sustained load: 7.5/5.0/3.5/2.5 MPa at 23 °C for 1 h/24 h/30 d/1 y, 40 °C rounds up to the 55 °C row; softening_c = 55 °C hard ceiling; 0.55 across-layer derate);
- **(c)** an implicit-only feature (thread/TPMS/organic blend, returned via HybridFuse route exact) AND/OR ≥2 catalog hardware items via the catalog/hole-wizard path;
- **(d)** a kinematic function with free-motion proofs (sweeps or posed stations) plus measured-interference negative controls in posed failure attitudes, each paired with a measured legal twin;
- **(e)** a real optimization (`param_optimize`, v2 command evaluator, receipts gated) whose constraints genuinely fight the objective;
- **(f)** where an internal air network exists, connectivity proven by the air-topology/genus audit with a must-fail control.

A card must meet at least four criteria including (a) and (b), be a genuinely complicated part (mechanism or geometry, not paperwork), keep every claim inside the engine's honest analysis surface, and record its mandatory refusals as rows, not silence.

## Cross-slate consistency rules (skeptic cross-cutting notes, binding)

1. **Buckling consistency:** the PLA transition-slenderness line λ_c = π·√(E/σ_y) = π·√(3300/55) ≈ 24 appears in every dossier that runs or skips `ace_buckling`. Aerospace runs it at L/r ≈ 46 (elastic range *for PLA*, unlike steel); robotics runs it at L/r ≈ 87; marine and biomedical skip it for stocky members citing the same arithmetic.
2. **No quiet validity drift:** no solver receives a load kind or motion its card excludes. The three drift cases the skeptic caught (biomedical slider → ace_contact, marine bore-pressure → tet, electronics rotation → translating obstacle) are repaired in the cards below, before any receipt is generated — not in errata.
3. **Every gate must be able to fail:** the two can't-fail gates as originally posed (energy balance-check on symmetric geometry, optics Hertz-contact stress) are re-posed below with can-fail replacements.
4. **Source-before-headline ledger** — numbers marked to-be-sourced that must land before their dependent claims ship: CubeSat 300 N/rail preload + GEVS/LV 100 Hz cites; DIN 878 measuring force; NASA-STD-3000/Peebles finger-push strength; ISO 9409-1:2004 flange tables + 25T horn manufacturer drawing; catalog spring geometries (biomedical, electronics, optics); Eminence PSD2002S-8 driver mass; Fillauer M103 / Ottobock 10V36 drawings; GPI 70-450 glass-finish drawing; OE PCD fitment tables.

## 1. aerospace — `cubesat_1u_dev_frame`

**What.** A 1U CubeSat development structure: the four-rail primary frame of a 100 × 100 × 113.5 mm CubeSat per the Cal Poly CubeSat Design Specification, with integrated PC/104 board-stack card guides, a deployment-switch pocket, M3 heat-set hardware bosses, harness/vent channels through the rail standoffs, and graded gyroid TPMS shear panels spanning between the rails. Users are CubeSat teams (university programs, startups) who print it for dispenser fit-checks, FlatSat-to-form-factor integration, HIL bench work, and bench vibration rehearsal before committing to machined aluminum. Explicitly NOT a flight article: PLA, room-temperature bench use, claims say so everywhere. **Framed from day one as a gauge-verified fit-check article** (mandatory, per repair R2 below).

**Mechanism.** The frame is a load-path machine, not a static box: per CDS, neighboring CubeSats and the dispenser pusher spring bear ONLY on the four 8.5 mm rail ends, so the rails are simultaneously a column-buckling case, a strength case, and the stiffness backbone that sets the first hard-mounted natural frequency. The kinematic function is the PC/104 avionics stack sliding into internal card guides along Z (sweep-proven insertion of a dimensioned board gauge) plus deployment-switch plunger seating at a rail end. The geometry is genuinely hard because tolerance-critical exact surfaces (rail envelope, rail-end pads, card-guide slots, switch pocket) must coexist with implicit-only TPMS shear panels and organic rail-to-standoff blends, fused via HybridFuse route exact so the gated rail faces survive verbatim; fuse-first/cut-last pillow discipline and the is_one_body connectivity oracle are load-bearing with lattice panels meeting thin rails.

**Interfaces.**
- Dispenser rail interface — CubeSat Design Specification (CDS) Rev 14.1, Cal Poly SLO: 8.5 mm min square rails, 100.0 ± 0.1 mm envelope, 113.5 mm height, rail-end pads, rail edge radius/chamfer; clearance fit into dispenser pocket, proven with tolerance_stack.py at ±0.15 mm FDM extremes against a printed pocket-section gauge. **The stack now carries an explicit first-layer flare / elephant-foot term** (repair R2); honest outcome may be that CDS ±0.1 envelope is NOT guaranteed as-printed — reported, not laundered.
- PC/104 board stack — PC/104 Specification (PC/104 Consortium) mechanical drawing: 90 × 96 mm board outline, 4× 3.2 mm corner mounting holes at dimensioned positions; card-guide slot running clearance ≥ 0.3 mm designed, tolerance-stacked at extremes; clearance fit.
- Fasteners/inserts — ISO 4762 M3 socket-head cap screws in ISO 273 medium 3.4 mm clearance holes plus Ruthex M3 heat-set inserts via catalog heatset_spec and heatset_insert_boss; clearance + insert interference per spec tables.
- Deployment switch pocket — subminiature snap-action switch per Omron SS-5GL (or D2F) datasheet outline, body and lever envelope plus 2× M2/M2.5 mounting holes; locational clearance fit, stacked at extremes.

**Load cases.**
- Rail-end stack compression: dispenser preload envelope ~300 N per rail (**source-before-headline**: CDS/P-POD user-guide qualification loads plus pusher-spring data) — strength via ace_fea, stability via ace_buckling.
- Stiffness screen: first hard-mounted natural frequency ≥ 100 Hz (**source-before-headline**: NASA-STD-GEVS / LV user-guide secondary-payload stiffness class) via ace_modal with clamped rail-end fixtures.
- Bench handling quasi-static: 1.33 kg CDS-max stack mass at 10 g on the standoffs/bosses (GEVS-derived quasi-static class) via ace_fea.
- Stowed creep: pusher-spring preload ~30–50 N held 30 days in a dispenser → rail stress gated against creep_allowable_mpa(23 °C, 720 h) = 3.5 MPa, duration stated; across-layer sections take the 0.55 factor explicitly.
- Optional bench-thermal: 2 W avionics dissipation conducted to ambient with user-supplied Robin film, hot side < softening_c = 55 °C (in-house-gated solver, stated as NOT registered).

**Analysis plan.**
- ace_modal (Validated) → is the first hard-mounted mode ≥ 100 Hz? Clamped rail-end fixtures (no free-free ambiguity), homogeneous isotropic PLA at RT is inside the card; band +1–3 % high so gate with margin below the raw number; **voxel pinned at 0.3 mm so the 1.2 mm TPMS strut floor = 4 voxels — one number for every voxel gate in the campaign** (repair R3); mode families identified via participation receipts, never a bare frequency list.
- ace_fea hex8 (Validated) → rail and standoff von Mises under 300 N/rail and 10 g stack; small-strain linear elastic valid because working stresses sit far below 55 MPa yield; nominal stresses accurate <1 %; any filleted junction peak taken as closed-form Kt × FEA nominal (voxel peaks do not converge to Kt); margins via production_check.py including the ~20 % deflection under-read.
- ace_buckling (Validated) → rail column eigenvalue under rail-end compression, mandatory 0.5 knockdown, gate on design_critical_load_n. **Validity justification printed in the dossier** (repair R1): λ_c = π·√(E/σ_y) ≈ 24 for PLA, so L/r ≈ 46 IS elastic-buckling range for this material even though it would be stocky in steel; the eigen job runs on the **full panel-braced frame**, not an isolated column (an isolated-column model gets the effective length wrong); the buckling gate stays PAIRED with the ace_fea strength gate, and the strength gate governs.
- param_optimize (Validated) → drives ace_modal + ace_fea receipts for the mass-vs-frequency trade (see optimization plan).
- Creep gate (cataloged arithmetic) → sustained rail stress ≤ creep_allowable_mpa(23 °C, 720 h) = 3.5 MPa, duration and temperature stated on the part.
- tolerance_stack.py → every interface above at ±0.15 mm FDM extremes **plus the first-layer flare term on the rail envelope** (repair R2, with raft + post-facing specified as the recovery option); rail-envelope stack reported honestly even if it fails CDS ±0.1.
- ace_thermal (in-house gated, NOT registered — stated) → optional 2 W conduction + film bench case, hot side < 55 °C; no convection network claimed.
- MANDATORY REFUSALS RECORDED: random-vibration response/PSD amplitudes (modal solver is undamped — no amplitude claims); thermal-vacuum/radiation (fenced); any flight qualification (physical test — bold required, NOT performed row); across-layer fatigue (must-refuse for all materials); no CFD/aero claims of any kind.

**Optimization plan.** param_optimize (Validated) over named parameters {rail wall thickness, TPMS panel grading coefficients, standoff web thickness}: minimize printed mass subject to first hard-mounted mode ≥ 100 Hz (ace_modal receipt), rail stress margin ≥ 0 at 300 N (ace_fea receipt + production_check), and buckling design_critical_load_n ≥ rail load. The trade is real: every gram removed from webs and panel density drops the first mode toward the 100 Hz floor, so the constraint binds and the optimizer must stop against it, not drift to a corner. Fallback enrichment: graded_infill of the panel interiors from the ace_fea stress field on the pinned voxel frame.

**Implicit features.** Graded gyroid TPMS shear panels between the rails (density graded toward rail junctions) and organic smooth-union blends at rail-to-standoff junctions — neither constructible in the exact B-rep half. Fused into the exact rail/guide body via HybridFuse route exact so tolerance-gated faces stay verbatim; pillow trap defended by datum probes on the rail envelope after every blend. Plus catalog hardware integration: ISO 4762 M3 screws and Ruthex M3 heat-set inserts through hole-wizard/heatset_insert_boss — criterion (c) met on both branches. **The TPMS here is structural (shear panels in the modal/buckling load path), which is why this card and robotics are the two slate members allowed to carry TPMS as criterion-(c) duty** (portfolio note).

**Negative controls.**
- Fat-rail twin: rail envelope grown +0.5 mm, posed in the printed dispenser pocket-section gauge → exact overlap_volume > 0 measured (must-not-fit proven); legal twin measured to clear ≥ 0.3 mm in the same pose.
- Oversized-board twin: 97 mm wide PC/104 gauge posed in the card guides → measured interference; legal 90 × 96 board gauge sweep-proven to insert full travel.
- Switch wrong-attitude control: SS-5GL body posed rotated 90° in its pocket → measured interference; correct attitude measured to seat with designed clearance.
- Support-free claim control: a deliberately unchamfered rail-end variant must FAIL the support_report steep_area == 0 gate (a gate that cannot fail is not a gate).

**Print story.** Printed standing on the rail ends: Z along the 113.5 mm axis, footprint 100 × 100 well inside the 256 mm bed. Rails are vertical prisms; all rail-to-standoff transitions carry 45° chamfers; gyroid panels are self-supporting at this orientation with struts floored at 1.2 mm = 4 voxels at the pinned 0.3 mm voxel (repair R3). **Because the CDS-critical rail-end pads are first-layer surfaces in this orientation, the print spec includes the first-layer flare term in the envelope stack and offers raft + post-facing of the rail-end pads as the compliant route** (repair R2). support_report gated steep_area == 0.0 exactly with the overhang threshold set at the printer limit, not a modelled face angle. wall_thickness gated flag_below 1.6 on structural walls. bridged_counterbore for any downward-facing bores; teardrop_hole for horizontal harness channels. PLA honesty: bench article at < 55 °C service stated on the part; short fit-checks are static cases, dispenser storage is declared a creep case gated at the 30-day table value; NO flight, vacuum, or thermal-cycling claims — recorded as refused/not-performed rows.

**Bar criteria.**
- (a) 4 mating interfaces with real published standards (CDS Rev 14.1, PC/104 spec, ISO 4762/ISO 273 + Ruthex, Omron SS-5GL datasheet), each with a tolerance_stack.py proof at ±0.15 mm extremes plus the first-layer term.
- (b) genuine numeric load path: 300 N/rail compression and first mode ≥ 100 Hz, honestly covered by ace_fea, ace_buckling (0.5 knockdown, paired strength gate, λ_c justification), and ace_modal within documented validity bands.
- (c) implicit-only features (graded TPMS shear panels, organic rail blends via HybridFuse) AND catalog hardware (M3 SHCS + heat-set inserts) — both branches.
- (d) kinematic function: PC/104 stack insertion sweep plus switch seating, with three measured-interference negative controls.
- (e) real optimization: mass minimization where the 100 Hz modal floor and rail strength/buckling gates genuinely fight the objective.
- (f) internal harness/vent channels proven connected by genus asserts, TPMS open-cell connectivity by the air-topology audit (no sealed voids).

**Risk.** The CDS envelope tolerance (±0.1 mm) is tighter than the house FDM tolerance (±0.15 mm), so the flagship tolerance stack may honestly conclude the printed frame cannot guarantee CDS envelope compliance without per-part measurement — the campaign is therefore framed as a gauge-verified fit-check article from day one (now mandatory, not optional). Secondary risk: a stiff 100 mm PLA frame may clear 100 Hz so easily that the modal constraint never binds; mitigation is to drive mass removal until the constraint is provably active, and if it still will not bind, switch the binding constraint to the rail buckling/creep gates and say so.

**Review repairs applied.**
- Skeptic R1: ace_buckling validity justified in-dossier via λ_c ≈ 24 (PLA elastic range extends to L/r ≈ 46); eigen job on the full panel-braced frame; strength gate governs; only design_critical_load_n quoted.
- Skeptic R2: first-layer flare/elephant-foot added as an explicit term to the rail-envelope stack; raft + post-facing specified; gauge-verified fit-check framing made mandatory.
- Skeptic R3: voxel inconsistency resolved — modal/FEA voxel pinned at 0.3 mm, TPMS strut floor 1.2 mm = 4 voxels everywhere.
- Judge: rated MARGINAL ("box with exceptional paperwork") but survives on interface density + the buckling/modal/creep triad + exact/implicit fusion difficulty; kept, with TPMS retained as genuinely structural (one of only two (c)-duty TPMS cards in the slate).
- 300 N/rail and 100 Hz entered in the source-before-headline ledger.

## 2. biomedical (orthotic/prosthetic/rehab/lab hardware) — `prosthetic_wrist_quick_disconnect`

**What.** A body-powered upper-limb prosthetic WRIST QUICK-DISCONNECT COUPLER: the forearm-side receptacle plus its terminal-device insert. The insert screws onto ANY standard terminal device (voluntary-opening hook, passive hand, tool adapter) through the universal 1/2"-20 UNF prosthetic TD thread; the receptacle swallows the insert stud, indexes it at 12 rotational positions (30° pronation/supination steps), retains it axially, and releases one-handed by pushing a transverse button on the radial face. The distal face bolts to a printed or laminated forearm plate through 4× M4 heat-set inserts. Users: transradial prosthesis users and the e-NABLE / open-prosthetics community who own a 1/2-20 hook but not a $400 Fillauer/Ottobock quick-disconnect wrist; also occupational-therapy clinics trialing terminal devices. Two printed pieces, one part campaign, plus catalog hardware. Explicitly a light-duty hobbyist device: NOT an ISO 22523-certified component and the dossier says so.

**Mechanism.** Two genuinely independent motions in one body. (1) AXIAL CAPTURE: the insert stud carries an annular retention groove; inside the receptacle a transverse push-button SLIDER carries a keyhole aperture — the large lobe passes the stud OD, the small lobe drops into the groove and captures it. Slider travel ~4 mm against a catalog compression spring; pressing the button aligns the large lobe and the TD falls out. (2) ROTATIONAL INDEXING: the insert flange OD carries 12 ramp-flanked notches; a printed cantilever detent finger flexes radially ~0.8 mm to ride from notch to notch, so the TD clicks into 30° increments and holds a stated torque, then overrides above a stated torque so a snag twists the hook rather than the socket. The hard geometry: the detent finger, slider slot, spring bore, TD bore and four mounting bosses all share one ~38 mm ring wall, so the beam that must be compliant enough to override at 20 N of finger push has to live 2 mm away from the shoulder that must not yield at 250 N of pull-out — and the notch ramp angle sets BOTH the holding torque and the insertion force, in opposite directions. Nothing here is two static pieces bolted together: remove the slider and the TD falls off; stiffen the detent and the user cannot rotate the hook.

**Interfaces.**
- 1/2"-20 UNF-2B internal thread in the TD insert — ASME B1.1-2019 Unified Inch Screw Threads; 2B minor/pitch/major limits researched (nominal 0.500 in / 12.7 mm, 20 tpi = 1.27 mm pitch). Threaded assembly, class 2B with an added FDM allowance on the minor diameter; stack at ±0.15 must still leave a thread that starts and does not strip. Confirmed by search that 1/2-20 is THE universal terminal-device thread (Fillauer QD wrist, Ottobock 10V36 Movo, N-Abler inserts).
- Insert stud OD ↔ receptacle bore — ISO 286-2 fit system via the in-tree iso286_fit lookup (target H8/f7 or H9/d9 running fit on ~16 mm nominal), then the house FDM allowance so designed running clearance ≥ 0.3 mm per OPERATOR_BRIEF §7. Running/rotating fit; it fights the indexing requirement (slop = index wobble).
- M4 heat-set insert boss (×4) on the distal mounting face — brass insert dimensions from the in-tree heatset_spec catalog (Ruthex-class M4) with heatset_insert_boss feature. Interference/melt fit. Mating screw: ISO 4762 M4×12 SHCS through an ISO 273 medium 4.5 mm clearance hole in the forearm plate.
- Compression spring pocket — stock music-wire compression spring, ASTM A228 wire, supplier catalog geometry researched (Lee Spring LC class, ~6.3 mm OD × ~12.7 mm free length). Slip fit in a blind bore, with a solid-height clearance check so the button cannot bottom the spring before the release lobe aligns. (**Source-before-headline**: catalog spring geometry/rate.)
- Distal spigot + 4-hole pattern to the forearm socket lamination ring — dimensioned interface researched from the Fillauer M103 Quick Disconnect Wrist manual (media.fillauer.com) and the Ottobock 10V36 lamination-ring drawing; body OD around 1-1/4 in per the Fillauer/N-Abler listings. Slip fit on the spigot, clearance on the bolt circle.

**Load cases.**
- L1 AXIAL PULL-OUT 250 N through the slider capture lobe and the insert groove shoulder. Estimate: 15 kg carried mass × 9.81 × 1.7 dynamic factor. Source-to-be: ISO 22523:2006 Annex A framework; if paywalled, the requirement is declared SELF-IMPOSED with the derivation printed, not laundered as a standard number.
- L2 ROTATIONAL INDEX HOLD ≥ 1.5 N·m, override ≤ 3.0 N·m at the detent notch. Source-to-be: published body-powered wrist rotation/friction torque figures (Ottobock 10V36 TDS, Fillauer M103 manual, UCLA O&P manual ch. 3).
- L3 ONE-HANDED RELEASE FORCE ≤ 20 N at the button, **scoped to the unloaded condition (no hanging TD)** — under load the lobe/groove friction contribution is untested capability, so loaded-release is recorded as indicative-only/unclaimed (repair R2). Source-to-be: 5th-percentile single-digit push strength (NASA-STD-3000 vol I / Peebles & Norris).
- L4 INSERTION PEAK FORCE ≤ 30 N over the full insert stroke (stud entering + detent snapping into a notch), so a user can seat the TD against the thigh.
- L5 SUSTAINED HANG 50 N (5 kg TD + carried object) held 8 h/day at 23 °C — a CREEP case, not a static one. Gate against creep_allowable_mpa(23 °C, 24 h) = 5.0 MPa, and ×0.55 = 2.75 MPa wherever the sustained path is across-layer. Source: tools/materials/pla.json creep table (the table governs; the legacy 0.2-fraction is a recorded conflict).
- L6 OFF-AXIS BENDING 4 N·m at the coupling face (5 kg at an 80 mm hook lever arm) — sized by hand first (tube 38/30 mm gives Z ~ 2.5e3 mm³ → ~1.6 MPa nominal) so the FEA is a check on the slot cutout, not a fishing trip.
- L7 SERVICE TEMPERATURE declared 5–45 °C ambient, worn under a sleeve. PLA softening_c = 55 °C; any claim at or above 55 °C is a condition violation and is refused, not designed for.

**Analysis plan.**
- tools/ace_fea_runner.py (ace_fea, Validated) — L1 250 N pull-out and L6 4 N·m bending on receptacle + insert. Hex8 linear elastic small-strain/small-displacement is exactly this case (peak demand well under yield; the slider/groove contact idealised as a bonded shoulder patch and SAID SO). Fixtures: clamped distal mounting face. Voxel 0.3–0.4 with ≥4 voxels across the 3 mm capture lobe. Deflection reads −6..−11 % low (converges from below) — quoted with that band. Loads checked against the >30 %-of-active-elements suspiciously-broad flag.
- CLOSED-FORM Kt × FEA NOMINAL for the annular groove root — mandated by the ace_fea card. Peterson annular-groove-in-tension Kt (cited) × FEA nominal (<1 % error). The voxel peak is reported as a cross-check only, never as the design number.
- tools/ace_fea_tet_runner.py (ace_fea_tet, Validated) — body-fitted tet10 re-run of the groove root as an independent read. Point loads only; fields are UNSTRUCTURED so used comparatively and never handed to graded_infill.
- tools/materials.py derated() + production_check.py — allowable for L1 is materials.derated('PLA', load_dir=Z, build_dir=Z, basis='yield') = 0.55 × 55 = 30.25 MPa (capture shoulder loads across layers in the chosen orientation). production_check.py on every FEA receipt; demand margined for the ~20 % under-read.
- tools/ace_contact_runner.py (in-house gated, NOT registered — stated every time) — **the index detent cantilever ONLY** (repair R1): corotational planar beam vs a translating rigid profile obstacle gives the notch engagement/override curve and path_max stress/strain over the stroke. Validity: PLANAR single-beam flexure, large rotation + small local strain; L/t ≥ 10; ramp geometry monotonic (no limit point — snap-through REFUSES); obstacle corners filleted. Baseline μ = 0; any μ > 0 run flagged UNTESTED CAPABILITY / indicative only.
- **Slider release/insertion statics (repair R1, replaces the former ace_contact slider row): closed-form wedge statics on the keyhole lobe chamfer + catalog spring rate + the solid-height check — cataloged-tier arithmetic, honestly labeled.** The slider is a quasi-rigid body on a catalog spring, not a beam flexure; running ace_contact on it would stretch the planar-beam card past its design case. L4's combined insertion peak = slider wedge share (cataloged) + detent share (ace_contact receipt), stated as a sum bound.
- tools/param_optimize.py (Validated) — see optimization plan; drives ace_contact_runner through the v2 command evaluator. ok:false evals are failed evals, never scored.
- tools/ace_fatigue_runner.py (in-house gated) — SCREENING ONLY on the detent finger's insert/remove cycles: PLA, in-plane, design curve (PS ≥ 90 %), R declared, ≤2e6 cycles, the 3.7×–90× life-scatter band quoted with every number, Kf by hand with the source's 16–29 % notch knockdown stated. Calibration-set caveats (100 % infill, RT, dry) recorded against the real part.
- MANDATORY REFUSAL #1 — cyclic fatigue of the axial capture shoulder: load_orientation is across_layer, which ace_fatigue REFUSES for ANY material. Recorded as refusal + required-NOT-performed row; the pull-out claim stays STATIC + creep only.
- tools/ace_thermal_runner.py (in-house gated, NOT registered) — steady conduction with user-supplied Robin film at 45 °C ambient: body stays below softening_c = 55 °C. Every connected component anchored by a BC. MANDATORY REFUSAL #2: solar/radiative gain (car dashboard) — fenced; recorded as required, NOT performed.
- MANDATORY REFUSAL #3 — ace_modal and ace_buckling NOT run, with the reason recorded: no dynamic/resonance requirement on a hand-carried coupler, and the button slider is a stocky column — **same λ_c = π·√(E/σ_y) ≈ 24 arithmetic as the aerospace and marine dossiers: L_eff/r sits far below even the PLA elastic-transition line, so it yields before it buckles** (cross-slate rule 1); the buckling card itself says pair with a strength gate and refuse stocky columns.
- MANDATORY REFUSAL #4 — no ISO 22523 compliance claimed anywhere; the standard is cited as the framework only; part labelled light-duty/non-certified.
- tools/tolerance_stack.py — all five interfaces at ±0.15 mm FDM extremes: thread minor/pitch, stud-to-bore running fit (must rotate at max material, must not wobble past index tolerance at least material), slider-in-slot, heat-set boss pilot, spring pocket + solid height.
- tools/sweep_check.py — free-motion proofs ONLY: slider 4 mm release stroke clears, insert rotates 360° through all 12 index positions, insert enters/exits along the bore axis with the slider pressed.
- Exact overlap_volume / clearance / assert_disjoint in POSED FAILURE ATTITUDES — the negative controls; must-NOT-fit claims live on exact booleans, never on sweeps.
- tools/air_topology_audit.py + genus asserts — spring blind bore vented to atmosphere (no hydraulic lock, cleanable); transverse slider slot has NOT broken into any of the four M4 boss bores.
- JSON-surface print + integrity gates: wall_thickness {flag_below: 1.6} judged on thin_area + p05_thickness; support_report {build_dir: Z} gated steep_area == 0.0 per orientation at the printer's real overhang limit; bounding_box [256,256,256]; exact_volume_within on every driven dimension; Mesh::is_one_body on both pieces; watertight receipt gated by hand on any voxel_healed route.

**Optimization plan.** tools/param_optimize.py (Validated) over four named parameters: detent beam free length L, beam thickness t, notch ramp angle α, notch depth d. OBJECTIVE: minimise the ace_contact insertion peak_force_n (L4 detent share, user comfort). CONSTRAINTS THAT FIGHT IT: (i) index holding torque ≥ 1.5 N·m — every change that lowers insertion force lowers holding torque through the same normal force and ramp; (ii) ace_contact path_max strain ≤ PLA elastic allowable over the whole stroke; (iii) the detent's HELD deflection stress ≤ creep_allowable_mpa(23 °C, 24 h) = 5.0 MPa, punishing the thin-beam solution the objective wants; (iv) beam root wall ≥ 1.6 mm printable. Driven through the v2 command evaluator calling ace_contact_runner.py plus a kernel-api program rebuild per candidate. A real Pareto fight — if the feasible band is EMPTY that is the campaign's honest headline result (state it, do not widen the target). SIMP/ace_optimize rejected with reason (envelope frozen by wrist OD + five interfaces; SIMP output is mesh-only and would destroy exact threaded/fitted faces). graded_infill rejected: no meaningful interior mass in a 38 mm ring.

**Implicit features.** BOTH arms of (c). IMPLICIT-ONLY: the 1/2"-20 UNF-2B internal thread (exact booleans on a helix self-intersect — thread_ridge / export_threaded or the implicit groove idiom, then HybridFuse route exact keeping exact seating faces verbatim). Voxel for a 1.27 mm pitch at the fine end of the table (0.06–0.12 mm), meshed in a tight local bound with narrow-band escape; the watertight receipt (not the table) wins; under-declared Lipschitz bounds trapped by switching to the manifold mesher and probing the datum. Pillow-trap discipline: fuse first, cut last, probe seating datums after any blend. HARDWARE (≥2 catalog items, independently): heatset_spec + heatset_insert_boss for 4× M4 brass inserts, ISO 4762 M4 screws with ISO 273 clearance holes, catalog ASTM A228 compression spring, bridged_counterbore on down-facing distal counterbores, teardrop_hole on the horizontal button bore, iso286_fit for the running-fit numbers.

- N2 LEGAL-PATH TWIN: identical pose with the slider pressed to full 4 mm travel — overlap_volume == 0 and minimum clearance ≥ 0.3 mm. The release path must measurably clear in the same run that N1 measurably interferes.

## 10. acoustics — `screw_on_exponential_horn` (kept on adjudication; hardened)

**Adjudication note (reviewers disagreed — decision and why).** The diversity judge scored this below the bar ("a funnel with two threads") and asked for a replacement concept, while the engine-fit skeptic PASSED it with one tooling repair and — critically — *verified* that its optimization corridor is real (mouth-limited cutoff ≈ 455 Hz at 240 mm vs the fc ≤ 700 Hz target: feasible and genuinely fought by bed and slope) and its load path honest. The slate keeps the horn, for four reasons. (1) Both runners-up are dead by their own cards and the judge concurs, so replacement means synthesizing an un-reviewed concept. (2) The judge's suggested replacement class — iris/variable-geometry acoustic devices — fails criterion (b) exactly the way the already-killed reflex port did: structurally unloaded moving leaves whose acoustic payoff is fenced aeroacoustics; swapping into a documented kill-class to gain mechanism points is a worse trade than keeping a doubly-audited card. (3) The horn's (b) is the *strongest* available in this domain: a ~1.55 kg steel driver cantilevered on a printed 27 TPI thread for years is a genuine creep-sizing problem (0.6 N·m sustained vs the 2.5 MPa 1-y cell), which no iris or port owns. (4) By the letter of the frozen bar the horn meets all six criteria. The judge's richness objection is answered by hardening (b) and (d) below and is retained honestly in the risk row: this is the slate's least mechanism-rich member, carried by geometry difficulty (dual fine-pitch implicit threads — the least-exercised tooling path — pillow-trap blends, and the folklore air-audit case) and by an acoustic receipt the blessed derived-model tier can actually deliver.

**What.** A rear-mount exponential mid/high-frequency horn (waveguide) for standard screw-on PA compression drivers: the driver threads into a 1-3/8"-27 TPI throat collar, the horn flares to a ~220–240 mm round mouth, and the body carries a 5/8"-27 mic-stand boss underneath plus four heat-set-insert baffle lugs. Target users are DIY loudspeaker builders and measurement hobbyists who own a screw-on driver (Eminence PSD2002S-8, GRS PT2522-8 class) and want a printable horn with a stated, receipt-backed cutoff frequency instead of a guessed STL from a file-sharing site.

**Mechanism.** Three threads of difficulty, with the sustained-load collar promoted to the headline engineering claim. (1) The throat collar is a real internal 1-3/8"-27 thread that must carry a ~1.5 kg cantilevered driver for years: thread engagement is a helical motion (proven at posed stations — see repair), seat depth is controlled by a shoulder stop, and the sustained gravity moment on the collar is a creep case, not a static one — THE design-driving load. (2) The flare is a mathematically-driven exponential surface r(x) = r_t·e^(mx) whose flare constant is not stylistic: it sets the acoustic cutoff via the cited Webster-equation closed form, and it fights the printer (wall slope), the bed (mouth diameter), and the structure (thin shell rings) — rib placement is driven by measured modal participation, a design input, not decoration. (3) The horn's function lives entirely in the internal air column — exactly the class where every material-side gate can smile while the channel is sealed, so the air-topology audit is load-bearing.
