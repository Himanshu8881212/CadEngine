# squatchee_spin — assembly instructions

## Steps

1. Snap the MOUNT over the cap's top button: center the six fingers on the button, press straight down until every finger clicks under the button rim. Fingers flex up to 2.9 mm (radius 1.5 mm each side) on a 19 mm button - PETG stays under 2% strain.
2. Drop the PROPELLER onto the mast, flat (bed) side down. It rests on the D9.2 boss shoulder; the D7.2 bore is roomy over the solid post BY DESIGN - the retainer sleeve, not the post, is its bearing.
3. Drop the RETAINER into the propeller bore with its two side windows over the post's lug nubs (rotate it until it slides fully home), then TWIST ~35 degrees until it stops - the lugs lock behind the window ledges. Confirm free spin: 0.3 mm radial play on the sleeve and 0.65 mm vertical float are by design. To remove for prop swaps: press down lightly, twist back, lift.
